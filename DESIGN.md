# Interface rules

- Lead with the action, cost and result. No decorative category labels or explanatory subtitles.
- Show freshness prominently and put screen collection next to it.
- Hide unavailable sales statistics and all-empty columns. Never invent quantities or sale times.
- Recipe reading order is one column: quantity and sale price, ingredients, then adjacent cost/profit totals. No detached sale sidebar. Put graphs and formulas behind a disclosure.
- Recalculate on input. A missing price preserves a labeled known subtotal and points directly to the missing field; never leave a priced ingredient list without a subtotal.
- Show ether comparison when it changes the cost. Keep item names and real icons.
- Tables support header sorting and persistent favorites pinned first.
- Do not ask for inventory counts: inventory capture is not implemented.
- Keep price estimates visibly approximate; explain assumptions on demand.
